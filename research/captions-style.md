# Burned-in caption styling for vertical shorts (2025–2026)

Scope: YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels, with a focus on faceless true-crime / mystery storytelling. All px values assume a 1080x1920 canvas.
Legend: **[S]** = stated by a source (URL given inline); **[I]** = my inference / synthesis, not directly sourced.

Caveat on sources: nearly all available material is vendor blogs (caption tools, AI editors). They repeat each other and a few numbers (engagement lifts, "% use this style") are marketing claims with no methodology. Treat them as industry consensus, not measured fact.

---

## 1. Findings

### 1.1 Fonts
- **Hormozi style**: Montserrat Black (900), ALL CAPS; Anton / Bebas Neue named as alternatives [S] ([Ascynd](https://ascynd.io/en/blog/hormozi-captions), [Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/blog/how-to-make-alex-hormozi-captions), [SendShort](https://sendshort.ai/guides/hormozi-captions/)).
- **MrBeast style**: Komika (Komika Axis), uppercase [S] ([Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/blog/how-to-make-captions-like-mrbeast)).
- **By niche** [S] ([Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok)): business/motivation → Montserrat Bold / Anton / The Bold Font, ALL CAPS; lifestyle → Poppins / Inter, sentence case; fitness/hype → Bebas Neue; news/commentary → Roboto, sentence case, thin outline, static; storytelling → Poppins, mixed case, typewriter reveal.
- Montserrat Bold is called "the go-to" for Shorts/Reels/TikTok; "bold white sans-serif, thin dark outline, 2–5 words, lower-middle" called a near-solved problem [S] ([Recapo](https://recapo.ai/blog/best-caption-styles-for-shorts/)). Recapo's genre table: documentary → neo-grotesque, minimal/no highlight, "calm, authoritative"; hype → condensed heavy display (Anton, Oswald).
- For storytelling/horror/mystery, Submagic recommends **DM Serif Display**; The Bold Font / Rubik for "strong, profound" messages [S] ([Submagic fonts](https://www.submagic.co/blog/best-font-for-subtitle)). Gothic-inspired fonts suggested for true crime/horror as accents [S] ([search summary of Async/Submagic articles](https://async.com/blog/the-best-font-for-captions/)).
- Bold (700+) fonts claimed to score "31% higher on mobile readability tests" [S, unverified claim] ([Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok)).
- **[I]** No source documents what the large faceless true-crime channels actually use. From the consensus above: heavy geometric sans (Montserrat 800–900) dominates both business and storytelling Shorts; the genre difference is in color/animation restraint, not font. Serif (DM Serif Display) is a credible differentiator but is thinner and less legible at speed.

### 1.2 Size, case, spacing
- Hormozi: **80–120 px**, ALL CAPS, letter-spacing **0 to −2%**, ~10–15% of frame height per line [S] ([Ascynd](https://ascynd.io/en/blog/hormozi-captions)).
- "Bold highlight" style: 55–75 pt on 1080x1920 [S] ([Blitzcut styles](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-style-tiktok)); OpusClip "bold statement" 60–80 pt, "minimal clean" 40–50 pt [S] ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-caption-presets-styles-boost-retention)).
- Case: sentence case, or ALL CAPS in short bursts [S] ([Recapo](https://recapo.ai/blog/best-caption-styles-for-shorts/)). ALL CAPS is standard for Hormozi/MrBeast/hype; sentence case for lifestyle/news [S] ([Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok)).
- Line height: no source gives a value. Captions.ai exposes line height/spacing controls [S] ([Captions.ai](https://captions.ai/help/docs/captions/styles)).

### 1.3 Stroke, shadow, box, colors
- Hormozi: fill **#FFFFFF**, stroke **#000000**, stroke **8–12 px**, emphasis **#FFD93D** (yellow), alt **#39FF14** (green), no shadow [S] ([Ascynd](https://ascynd.io/en/blog/hormozi-captions)). Other sources give yellow **#F7C204** and green **#02FB23** [S] ([Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok)) or green **#22C55E** [S] (animatecaptions, via [search](https://animatecaptions.com/hormozi-captions)).
- MrBeast: black stroke with "high" width, green keywords with glow/shadow [S] ([Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/blog/how-to-make-captions-like-mrbeast)).
- General: white fill, **thin dark stroke 3–6 px**, soft drop shadow for bright/busy footage, semi-transparent box only if the outline isn't enough [S] ([Recapo](https://recapo.ai/blog/best-caption-styles-for-shorts/)). Minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1 [S] ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-caption-presets-styles-boost-retention)).
- Background box ("subtitle block"): 3–5 words in a semi-transparent box, for tutorials [S] ([Blitzcut styles](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-style-tiktok)).
- Dated: light text without outline, script fonts for long captions, small fonts, inconsistent styling [S] ([Blitzcut styles](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-style-tiktok)).

### 1.4 Words per page / lines
- Hormozi: 1–3 words per beat, exactly one highlighted word [S] ([Ascynd](https://ascynd.io/en/blog/hormozi-captions)). MrBeast: generally 2 words per line [S] ([Submagic](https://www.submagic.co/blog/how-to-make-captions-like-mrbeast)).
- Consensus: 2–5 words on screen [S] ([Recapo](https://recapo.ai/blog/best-caption-styles-for-shorts/)). OpusClip: 5–8 words ideal on mobile, 8–12 max, caption duration 1.5–3 s, 150–250 ms gap between captions [S] ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-caption-presets-styles-boost-retention)) — this is a more subtitle-like, less punchy standard.
- Captions.ai exposes words-per-line, lines-per-page, max characters and pause-split settings [S] ([Captions.ai](https://captions.ai/help/docs/captions/styles)).
- **[I]** At 84 px Montserrat ExtraBold caps (~0.68 em average advance, ~57 px/char), an 800 px wide box fits ~14 characters per line.

### 1.6 Highlight techniques — current vs. dated, tone fit
- Word-by-word / short-phrase with white text + black outline is described as the most widely used 2026 style across niches, but "overuse leads to viewer fatigue — use for hooks only" [S] ([Blitzcut styles, via search](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-style-tiktok)).
- Recapo: apply word-by-word highlighting for dense, fast speech (podcasts, hype); **skip it for slow, atmospheric, cinematic footage**; documentary → minimal/no highlight [S] ([Recapo](https://recapo.ai/blog/best-caption-styles-for-shorts/)).
- Highlight variants in tools [S]: active-word color (Hormozi, OpusClip "Karaoke"), active-word background pill (Captions.ai "active word background"), auto scale / random rotation (Captions.ai, Hormozi "rotation and size changes" per [SendShort](https://sendshort.ai/guides/hormozi-captions/)), emoji insertion (Captions.ai; OpusClip says max one emoji per line, claims +15–25% for entertainment/lifestyle), AI keyword emphasis (Captions.ai, VEED "Emphasize words with AI"), typewriter reveal named an emerging storytelling trend ([Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok)).
- Tool preset names [S]: Submagic — Hormozi 1–5, Beast, Karl, Ella, Leila, Umi, etc. ([Submagic API](https://docs.submagic.co/api-reference/templates)); OpusClip — Karaoke, Beasty, Deep Diver, Pod P, Mozi, Popline, Glitch Infinite, Seamless Bounce ([OpusClip search](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-caption-presets-styles-boost-retention)); VEED dynamic — Glide, Glide2, Pulse, Whisper, Fusion, Glass, Terminal, Handwritten ([VEED help](https://support.veed.io/en/articles/12000003-how-to-use-dynamic-subtitles)); CapCut — Glow, Trending, Aesthetic ([CapCut](https://www.capcut.com/resource/caption-style)); Captions.ai — 30+ templates, names not published in docs.
- **[I]** For a dark/serious tone: emoji, random rotation, bouncy overshoot, rainbow/multi-color and Hormozi yellow/MrBeast green read as hype/business and undercut gravity. Suitable: a single restrained accent color used sparingly on key nouns (names, dates, "dead", "missing"), a small non-bouncy scale on the active word, or a word-by-word reveal (suspense pacing). A pill/box behind the active word is legible but reads "podcast clip".

### 1.7 Timing and animation
- Hormozi: instant snap or subtle scale-up **≤105%**, 200–500 ms per word [S] ([Ascynd](https://ascynd.io/en/blog/hormozi-captions)); "each word scales up with a subtle bounce" [S] ([search summary](https://animatecaptions.com/hormozi-captions)).
- **Captions lead audio**: highlight each word **50–100 ms before speech**, hold full word duration + 50–100 ms; caption pages lead audio by **100–200 ms** [S] ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/best-caption-presets-styles-boost-retention)).
- Sync tolerance: viewers rarely notice errors under ~100 ms; aim ±50 ms for pro work [S] ([search summary: subtitle timing guides](https://waywithwords.net/resource/accurate-caption-timing-for-your-videos/)).
- Minimal/lifestyle presets use fades; fitness uses fast bounce; news static [S] ([Blitzcut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok)).

## 2. Recommended spec for a dark true-crime short

All **[I]**: synthesis of the sourced ranges above, tuned toward the restrained/documentary end.

- **Font**: Montserrat ExtraBold 800 (fallback Black 900), UPPERCASE, 84 px, letter-spacing −0.01em, line-height 1.05.
- **Fill** #FFFFFF; **stroke** 8 px visible #000000 outside glyph (CSS `-webkit-text-stroke:16px #000; paint-order:stroke fill`); **shadow** `0 6px 18px rgba(0,0,0,.55)`; no box.
- **Keyword accent** #E0342F, max one word per page (names, dates, places, loaded words); keeps stroke.
- **Pages**: 2–4 words (target 3), ≤14 chars/line, ≤2 lines; break at punctuation or pauses ≥250 ms; never split a name; min 12 frames on screen.
- **Layout**: centered, max width 800 px (x 140–940), block centered at **y = 1150 px** (clears all bottom UI; lowest limit 1436).
- **Highlight**: word-by-word reveal in a pre-laid-out page (unspoken words opacity 0, no reflow); active word pops scale 1.10→1.00 over **4 frames**, ease-out cubic, no bounce/rotation; reveal leads audio by **2 frames**. No active-word color change.
- **Page entrance**: opacity 0→1 over 3 frames + the pop; page leads first word audio by 3 frames. **Exit**: hard cut; if silence ≥0.6 s, fade out over 4 frames starting 6 frames after the last word.
- **Hook**: first 2–3 s may use 1–2 words/page at 110 px.
- No emoji, rotation, bounce or multi-color.
